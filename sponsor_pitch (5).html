What is a television billboard? A billboard is a short sponsor announcement broadcast during the programme: "This programme is brought to you by…" — not a roadside advertisement. It is a well-established and trusted format on Dutch television for brand association with quality editorial content.
All content is produced within an agreed editorial script. Your brand is positioned in the context of real applications and market developments — not in advertising language. Filming is arranged flexibly in consultation.
